Destination Guides

Know Before You Go

Practical guides to help you plan, and to capture the curiosity of every traveller searching for their next great adventure.

How to Travel to Maasai Mara

The Mara is 270 km from Nairobi, reachable by road in 5–6 hours or a scenic 45-minute flight. The best wildlife viewing runs from July through October during the Great Migration.

  • Daily flights from Wilson Airport
  • Peak season: Jul – Oct
  • Avg. temp: 12 – 28 °C

Amboseli: Land of Giants

Amboseli National Park sits at the foot of Mount Kilimanjaro and hosts over 1,600 elephants. Its flat, open terrain makes it one of Africa's most photogenic parks.

  • 4 hrs drive from Nairobi
  • Best for photography
  • Year-round water sources

Samburu & the Northern Frontier

Kenya's arid north offers a completely different safari — rare species, Samburu culture, and landscapes that feel untouched by time. Ideal for repeat visitors seeking something new.

  • Unique wildlife species
  • Rich cultural encounters
  • Boutique bush camps
